# FriendGroupList Rules

## Level Requirements

• Must be created by a member of the FriendGroup.
• Must have a legitimate verification.
• Must be possible on 60 FPS.
• Joke levels may be removed at staff discretion.

## Placements

Placements are decided based on:
- Community opinion
- Completion difficulty
- Existing list placements

## Updates

Major buffs or nerfs may result in rerates or complete removal.

## Removal

Levels may be removed if:
- They become impossible.
- Verification is proven illegitimate.
- They no longer meet list standards.
