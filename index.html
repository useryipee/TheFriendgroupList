List Guidelines

Every submission, run, and profile on this site is held to these four rules.
  • 01
    All records must be approved by Useryipee Submit your run with proof and wait for it to be reviewed. Unapproved runs are not added to the list and won't count on the leaderboard. Required for every submission
  • 02
    Proof is mandatory A raw, unedited clip or screenshot showing the completion (attempt count and progress visible) must come with every submission. No proof means no record, no exceptions. No proof, no record
  • 03
    Legit runs only No hacks, noclip, speedhacks, or any form of cheating. Getting caught voids every record you've ever submitted and gets you banned from future submissions. Zero tolerance
  • 04
    Your flag(s) must reflect your own heritage Pick the country or countries you're actually from or connected to by birth, citizenship, or family - not just a flag you think looks cool. Applies to every profile
